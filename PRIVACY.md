## What the app does

Dark Mode Safari is a Safari extension that changes webpage colors on your Mac. All theme features are free. No developer account is required.


## Webpage content and permissions

Safari website access lets the extension read page styles, images and page structure to create a theme. This processing happens on your Mac. The app does not send your browsing history, page content, search queries or site settings to the developer. Private-window operation uses the same local processing. On macOS 14 or later, it requires a separate Safari private-window permission.

To render a website, Safari may request that site’s stylesheets and images from their original servers, including resources the extension needs to examine. Those servers receive ordinary web requests under their own privacy policies. The app does not operate a browsing proxy or developer content server.


## Data stored on your Mac

Theme preferences, per-site rules, automation choices, settings and temporary extension state are stored locally. Manually entered sunset coordinates stay local; the app does not request device location. The app can retain local usage dates, support-reminder timing and a flag that you have supported the app to manage optional support invitations. These values are not sent to the developer. There is no app-managed cloud sync.

Settings export creates a file you choose to save; imports read the file you choose. Exported files can contain website rules and coordinates. You control whether to keep or share them. Local preferences may remain after uninstall under macOS and Safari storage behavior. Exported files remain until you delete them.


## Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs or developer data-collection backend. It does not collect personal data for the developer.


## Optional tips

Apple handles optional consumable support purchases using StoreKit. The app checks transaction verification on your Mac and can record that support occurred locally. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple handles purchase data under Apple’s own policies. Tips do not unlock features and are not subscriptions.


## This support website and voluntary messages

This support site contains no analytics scripts, cookies or tracking pixels added by the developer. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes requests under [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Opening a GitHub issue is optional and public. Any text or attachments you post are visible to the developer and others; GitHub handles account and website data under its own policies. Do not post private browsing history or sensitive information.


## Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/bahadiryl/dark-mode-safari-support/issues) without including sensitive information. You can also email info@iyonya.app for private support or privacy requests. If you contact us by email, we use the email address, message and attachments you choose to send to respond to your request, retaining them only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. This policy will be updated if the app’s handling of data changes.

## Uygulamanın işlevi

Dark Mode Safari, Mac’inizde sayfa renklerini değiştiren bir Safari uzantısıdır. Tüm tema özellikleri ücretsizdir. Geliştirici hesabı gerekmez.


## Sayfa içeriği ve izinler

Safari site erişimi, uzantının tema oluşturmak için sayfanın stillerini, görsellerini ve yapısını okumasını sağlar. Bu işlem Mac’inizde yapılır. Uygulama tarama geçmişinizi, sayfa içeriğini, arama sorgularını veya site ayarlarını geliştiriciye göndermez. Özel pencerelerde de aynı yerel işlem kullanılır. macOS 14 ve üstünde Safari’den ayrıca özel dolaşma izni gerekir.

Safari, sayfayı oluşturmak için uzantının incelemesi gereken kaynaklar dahil sitenin stil ve görsellerini özgün sunucularından isteyebilir. Bu sunucular normal web isteklerini kendi gizlilik politikaları kapsamında alır. Uygulama bir tarama vekil sunucusu veya geliştirici içerik sunucusu işletmez.
